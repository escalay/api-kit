# Find your way

Learn what __title__ offers before the first real task, so the first call is the right one.

## Steps

1. Read `/llms.txt`: what the service is for, its words, and every endpoint and tool.
2. Over MCP, list the tools: each says when to use it. Over HTTP, `/openapi.json` has every route with an example.
3. Make one read-only call and follow the `_links` in its answer.

Done when you can name the tool or the route for the user's task.
